RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on September 1, 2024, by and between Apex Realty Partners LLC ("Landlord"), and Alex Rivera ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord the residential premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apartment 4B, Springfield ("Premises"). The term of this Lease shall begin on October 1, 2024, and shall terminate on September 30, 2025. Tenant must surrender possession upon termination.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $2,400.00 USD, payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. Payments must be made via electronic portal or certified check. If rent is not received by Landlord on or before 11:59 PM on the fifth (5th) calendar day of the month, Tenant shall pay an immediate late charge equal to ten percent (10%) of the overdue rent balance.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,800.00 USD as a security deposit. Landlord shall hold the security deposit and may apply funds to remedy non-payment of rent, repair damages, or deduct for any cleaning and normal wear and tear repairs upon vacancy. Landlord shall return the remaining balance within sixty (60) days following full surrender of the Premises, accompanied by an itemized deduction statement.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition at all times. Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, routine plumbing clogs, appliance repairs, and replacement costs for any repairs costing under $500.00 USD per incident. Landlord shall only be responsible for major structural defects and exterior roofing, provided written notice has been delivered by Tenant.

5. LANDLORD ENTRY AND ACCESS
Landlord and Landlord's authorized agents, contractors, and prospective buyers or mortgagees may enter the Premises at any time of day or night without prior written notice to inspect the condition of the Premises, perform alterations, or display the unit.

6. SUBLETTING AND OCCUPANCY
Tenant shall not assign this Lease, sublet any portion of the Premises, nor allow any license or occupancy by third parties without the prior express written consent of Landlord. Any overnight guest staying on the Premises for more than five (5) consecutive nights or ten (10) total nights within any 30-day period shall be considered an unauthorized occupant, triggering immediate default.

7. EARLY TERMINATION AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
If Tenant vacates, abandons, or terminates this Lease prior to the expiration of the natural term, Tenant shall remain strictly liable for the entire rent balance remaining through September 30, 2025. In addition, Tenant shall forfeit the full security deposit and pay a liquidated damages cancellation penalty of $3,000.00 USD within seven (7) days of vacancy.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND LEGAL FEES
In any dispute or litigation arising out of or related to this Agreement, Tenant expressly waives the right to a trial by jury and agrees that all disputes shall be settled by binding confidential arbitration. Tenant agrees that if Landlord initiates legal action and prevails, Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for all actual attorney fees and court costs incurred; however, under no circumstances shall Landlord be liable for Tenant's attorney fees.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND SEVERABILITY
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be severed and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
